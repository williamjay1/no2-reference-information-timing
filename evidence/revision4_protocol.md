# Fourth revision: sensor calibration information timing

## Routing and scope

Target: IEEE Sensors Journal, regular empirical SCIE sensor measurement/method paper. The main question is how reference event time, delivery time and legitimate same-site sharing change sequential NO2 correction at a fixed attempted-access budget. The contribution path is a sensor-calibration information design, tested with an independent GP active selector, a delay-aware covariance planner and an aligned component decomposition. Standard estimators are appropriate comparison tools; neither a complex architecture nor economic causal identification is a universal gate. Source data remain public QUANT processed concentration reports, not electrode voltages.

The reviewer must be able to assess information order, competitive same-task correction, sustained error, device heterogeneity and whether the acquisition objective predicts realized value. No global parameter identifiability, physical electrode mechanism, deployment cost saving or universal latency law will be claimed from these experiments.

## Revision status and inferential scope

This is an exploratory revision prompted after the original evaluation results were known. New tuning is restricted to earlier Manchester records ending before 2021-01-01; later records are rerun chronologically under frozen choices, but they are not a new untouched confirmation sample. Original data, scripts and validated outputs are retained. All new computation is on D:; immutable raw materials stay on F:.

## Experiments and ownership

Compare with identical selected GP periodic and AR-transferred fixed/variable calendars. The planning grid and quadrature are documented computational approximations. Owner: engineering_revision.
2. 72-hour delay-aware planner: explicitly simulate pending and future reference arrival order, propagate event-time covariance to output time, and choose horizon/threshold only on pre-2021 Manchester. Frozen candidate grid: horizon 168/240 hours crossed with threshold 1.0/1.25. Same calibrator, budget and fixed-window convention. Compare with original 72-hour delayed periodic and adaptive controllers on their exact all-delay common masks. Owner: root. Details: revision4_delay_protocol.json.
3. Sustained correction decomposition: adaptive/frozen correction coefficients crossed with persistent/white residuals under the same realized B48 fixed calendar and initialization. Frozen coefficient means use a zero-coefficient-gain Joseph/Schmidt update retaining commissioning uncertainty; white residuals retain stationary innovation variance. Full support and common completed-window age >=168-hour support. Owner: environment_diagnostics.
4. Coefficient and heterogeneous-response diagnostics: four coefficient trajectories in physical feature units, uncertainty and observed changes; leave-one-instrument-out, long/short record and site-balanced summaries; archive reference/context descriptors. These are descriptive and cannot attribute electrode aging. Owner: environment_diagnostics.
5. Targeted primary-source checks: Tancev 2021 and 2022, NO2 environmental/drift components, QUANT site/reference metadata, closest neural calibration architectures and limits of their uncertainty outputs. Owner: sensor_sources.

## Evaluation and exit gates

Primary metric: equal-weight mean physical-instrument RMSE; simultaneous channels average squared losses before RMSE, replacement segments pool within physical instrument. Pair all methods on identical scored masks. Report point contrasts and paired circular within-site-quarter calendar-block intervals, 7 days/1000 replicates and 14 days/300 replicates. Intervals are conditional on observed sites, instruments and realized fitted policies; no independent-hour normality assumption, no geographic-population or tuning uncertainty claim. Preserve unfavorable outcomes and separate a strategy contrast from a difference of strategy contrasts.

Required checks before writing: target and future-feature noninterference, prediction/action before reference delivery, exact attempted budgets/start cap, independent covariance or GP posterior check, new stream aggregation and original parity where appropriate. A reported finding must map to a saved numeric output. Draft Methods/Results before Discussion/Introduction/Abstract after the result ledger and article build specification are frozen. One consolidated requirements/claim audit replaces mechanical duplicate stage files, per the user's project instructions.
